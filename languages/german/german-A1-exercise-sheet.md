## 💬 Translation Exercise 1A — First Day at a Language School

*Translate this conversation into German.*

> **Teacher:** Good morning! My name is Müller.
> **Student:** Good morning! I'm sorry — how do you pronounce your name?
> **Teacher:** "Müller" — the "ü" is like saying "oo" but with rounded lips.
> **Student:** Ah, I understand. And the "ü" — is that a special character?
> **Teacher:** Yes, exactly. German has four special characters: ä, ö, ü, and ß.
> **Student:** Thank you very much!
> **Teacher:** You're welcome. German pronunciation is very consistent — once you learn the rules, you can read everything.

> **Student:** And "Lampe"?
> **Teacher:** "Die Lampe" — feminine. Words ending in "-e" are often feminine.
> **Student:** What about "Mädchen"? It means "girl" — is it feminine?

> **Teacher:** The golden rule: never learn a noun without its article!
